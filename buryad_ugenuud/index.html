Buryad Ugenuud

Let’s find the words for today, together.

New ideas need new words. Buryad Ugenuud brings Buryat speakers together to suggest translations, discuss them, and grow a shared vocabulary for the modern world.

Make room for new words

How do you say a new concept in Buryat? Start a translation poll, offer possible words, and invite the community to share its knowledge. When a poll is closed, word creators can save a translation so it can be found and used by everyone.

  • Suggest translations Create a poll for a word or idea that needs a Buryat translation.
  • Learn from the community Compare suggestions, vote, and discuss the options together.
  • Build a shared vocabulary Search saved translations and help make new words available to all.
