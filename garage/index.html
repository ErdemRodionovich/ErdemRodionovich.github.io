Garage

Calling All Logistics Center Managers!
Ready to take on a challenge and show off your management skills? Then this puzzle game is made just for you! In it, you'll become the head of a massive logistics center, where trucks deliver brand-new cars daily. Your mission is to sort them by color to ensure the warehouse is always perfectly organized. Sounds simple? Think again! Your moves are limited, and every mistake could cost you.
Forget boring puzzles; here, you'll find:

  • Engaging Gameplay: Plan every move, using logic and spatial reasoning.
  • Vibrant Graphics: Enjoy detailed models of trucks and cars that are just begging to be added to your collection.
  • Increasing Difficulty: From simple tasks to puzzles that will make your brain work at full throttle!
Can you become the most efficient logistics center manager and sort all the cars by color using the fewest possible moves? Download the game now and prove you're the best in the business!
